AI Lead Generation & Follow-Up Agent

Overview:-
We built this AI-powered automation agent during Azinhack 2026 to help businesses manage lead generation and follow-ups more efficiently. It helps businesses identify potential leads, categorize them based on their interest, and automate communication to improve lead conversion.

Problem Statement:-
Businesses often struggle to find quality leads and follow up with potential customers at the right time. Delayed or missed follow-ups can result in lost business opportunities.

Our Solution:-
Our AI agent automates parts of the lead management process by organizing incoming leads and triggering appropriate follow-up actions based on their interest level.

- Lead Management: Helps businesses organize incoming leads.
- Lead Classification: Categorizes leads as hot, warm, or cold based on their interest.
- Automated Follow-Ups: Initiates relevant communication for different lead categories.
- Business Outreach: Helps businesses respond to potential customers more efficiently.

Workflow:-
1. A potential lead submits a form.
2. The system processes the submitted information.
3. Leads are categorized based on their interest level.
4. Automated emails or follow-up actions are triggered according to the category.
5. Hot leads can be directed towards a meeting, while warm and cold leads receive suitable offers or demos.

Tech Stack:-
- n8n – Workflow automation
- APIs and Integrations – Connecting services and automating communication
- AI Agent / AI Model – Lead analysis and classification
- Gmail – Automated email communication
